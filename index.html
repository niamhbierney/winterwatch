Governance

Who decides? The Duty Engineer reviews the evidence and approves or rejects treatment. AI is advisory only and cannot authorise or dispatch work.

What is recorded? Record forecast values and source time, route decisions, depot stock and truck availability, planned departure times, the engineer's approval/rejection and any override reason or local observation.

This is a public coursework demonstration, not a secure council system. Do not enter confidential information.
